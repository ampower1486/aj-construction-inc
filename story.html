Our Story

A father, his two sons, and a crew that treats every home like it's their own.

Where it all started: our father's trade

Long before there was an AJ Construction Inc, there was a father who brought his two boys to work with him. Weekends, summers, every school break — carrying lumber, sweeping up, and slowly earning real responsibility as we proved we could handle it. Thirteen years later, we had our own tools, our own crews, and a name we are proud of across El Dorado County and Greater Sacramento. And through it all, our dad has stayed close — still the first call whenever a job needs another set of experienced eyes.

What he passed down to us was not just the trade — it was the standard behind it: show up when you say you will, leave the site clean, and tell a homeowner the truth, especially when the truth costs more than they hoped. That is the version of this business we grew up believing in, and it is the only version we build.

Every project we take on gets the same care we would want for our own family's home.
Our standard, on every job

So we built the company we are proud to stand behind

AJ Construction Inc holds a Class B General Building licence with the California State Licence Board — issued November 2024, licence #1129358, bonded to $25,000. That licence means we can take full responsibility for a project from foundation to finish, not just one trade, and it is something you can verify for yourself in under a minute.

We are still very much a family business, and that is by design. When you call, one of the owners answers. When we give you a price, it is because we are the ones doing the work. And if something needs attention along the way — because construction sometimes calls for that — you are talking directly to the family whose name is on the truck, not a call centre.

A family at the core, a team you can trust

Most days you will find us on the property — often all three together — one leading the crew, one walking you through what comes next, and our dad, whose instincts after a lifetime in this trade still catch details the rest of us would miss. Working alongside us is a crew we have built relationships with over years, tradespeople we trust with our own name and reputation.

That is what sets AJ Construction apart: the accountability of a family business, paired with the skill of an experienced team that has built decks, remodelled kitchens, poured concrete and put on roofs together season after season. Everyone on our crew holds the same standard we were raised on.

We are proud of the company we have built — proud of the name on the truck, and proud of the people who show up under it every day. When you hire AJ Construction Inc, you get a family that treats your project personally, and a team experienced enough to get it right the first time.

What we will not do

  • Give you a number over the phone without seeing the job.
  • Start work before you have the scope and price in writing.
  • Bill you for a change you did not approve first.
  • Cover up work that would not pass inspection.
  • Disappear with a deposit. The licence and the bond exist for exactly that reason.

How It Works

Four steps, no mystery

One of the owners comes out, looks at the actual conditions, and listens to what you are trying to end up with. Free, and no pressure to sign anything.

You get it in writing

A written scope and estimate covering the work, the materials and the price — itemised, so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

Our own crew, a start date we hold to, and a site that gets cleaned up at the end of every day. You always know who is in your house.
