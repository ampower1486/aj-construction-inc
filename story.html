Our Story

A father, his two sons, and one very simple reason for starting our own company.

We learned this trade from our dad

Before there was an AJ Construction Inc, there was a father taking his two boys to work. Weekends, summers, and every school holiday — carrying lumber, sweeping up, and slowly being handed real work as we proved we could be trusted with it. Thirteen years later the two of us had our own tools, our own crews and our own reputation across El Dorado County and Greater Sacramento — and our dad was still the first call whenever something on a job did not look right.

You see both versions of this trade in that time. You see crews who show up early, leave the site clean, and tell the homeowner the truth when something turns out to be expensive. And you see jobs where the price moves mid-week, where nobody can say who is coming tomorrow, and where the owner only finds out about the problems on the final invoice. Our father raised us on the first kind, and had no patience for the second.

We got tired of watching good people pay for work we would not have accepted in our own homes.
Why we started AJ Construction Inc

So we built the company we would want to hire

AJ Construction Inc took out its Class B General Building licence with the California State Licence Board in November 2024 — licence #1129358, bonded to $25,000. That licence is not decoration. It is what allows us to take responsibility for a whole house rather than one trade, and it is something anyone can verify in about thirty seconds.

We are still a family company, and that is deliberate. When you call, one of the owners picks up. When we give you a price, it is because we are the ones doing the work. And when something goes sideways — because in construction it sometimes does — you are dealing with the person whose family name is on the truck.

Three of us, one name on the truck

Not many families get to do this, and we do not take it lightly. On most days you will find all three of us on the same property — one running the crew, one walking a customer through what comes next, and our dad quietly putting right something none of us had spotted yet. A lifetime of instinct does not retire just because his sons can read a set of plans.

There is a kind of accountability that only exists when you work beside your father and your brother. Nobody cuts a corner quietly. Nobody talks to a customer in a way they would be embarrassed to repeat at the dinner table on Sunday. The name on the truck is our surname, and all three of us carry it home at the end of the day.

We are proud of that, and we think it shows in the work. When you hire AJ Construction you are not hiring a logo — you are hiring a father and his two sons, and every one of us has a reason to get your job right.

What we will not do

  • Give you a number over the phone without seeing the job.
  • Start work before you have the scope and price in writing.
  • Bill you for a change you did not approve first.
  • Cover up work that would not pass inspection.
  • Disappear with a deposit. The licence and the bond exist for exactly that reason.

How It Works

Four steps, no mystery

We walk it with you

One of the owners comes out, looks at the actual conditions, and listens to what you are trying to end up with. Free, and no pressure to sign anything.

You get it in writing

A written scope and estimate covering the work, the materials and the price — itemised, so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

We build it

Our own crew, a start date we hold to, and a site that gets cleaned up at the end of every day. You always know who is in your house.

We walk it again

Final walkthrough together. Anything you are not happy with goes on a punch list and gets handled before we call the job done.
