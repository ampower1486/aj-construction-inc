How It Works

Four steps, no mystery

You should know what happens next at every point in the job. This is the whole process.

We walk it with you

One of the owners comes out, looks at the actual conditions, and listens to what you are trying to end up with. Free, and no pressure to sign anything.

You get it in writing

A written scope and estimate covering the work, the materials and the price — itemised, so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

We build it

Our own crew, a start date we hold to, and a site that gets cleaned up at the end of every day. You always know who is in your house.

We walk it again

Final walkthrough together. Anything you are not happy with goes on a punch list and gets handled before we call the job done.
